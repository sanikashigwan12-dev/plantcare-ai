About AgriVision AI

AgriVision AI is an advanced digital platform designed to bridge the gap between artificial intelligence and sustainable agriculture. Our mission is to protect crops and help farmers diagnose plant diseases early before they cause significant yield loss.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower farmers, gardeners, and agricultural researchers with real-time, highly accurate disease detection using Computer Vision and Deep Learning technologies.

💡 Key Features

⚡ Real-Time Analysis

Upload a leaf picture and get instantaneous diagnostic results within seconds.

🧪 Targeted Cure Remedies

Provides exact treatment instructions, bio-fungicides, and organic remedies for detected infections.

🎯 High Accuracy AI

Powered by Deep Learning models trained on thousands of plant disease image datasets.

🖥️ Easy-to-Use UI

Clean, accessible, and Google Material-inspired user interface designed for everyone.

🛠️ Technology Stack

  • Backend: Python 3, Flask Web Framework
  • Machine Learning & Computer Vision: TensorFlow / Keras, OpenCV, NumPy
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 (Google Material Design System), JavaScript
  • Operating Environment: Linux Environment (CentOS / RedHat)

🌱 Why Early Detection Matters

Plant diseases affect crop yields severely each year. Early identification allows targeted use of treatments, reduces environmental impact, and minimizes economic loss for farmers worldwide.
